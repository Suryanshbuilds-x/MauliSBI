Research method

How to read this dashboard

What was checked

Official institute course pages, branch/contact pages, current batch listings where available, course descriptions, teacher information, and cross-checking directory entries. Official pages are treated as evidence of what an institute advertises — not independent proof that every feature is delivered in every current batch.

Key cautions

  • Paramount and JEC have the clearest published SBI-focused course detail; both still need classroom verification.
  • Sterling lists a separate SBI PO course and teacher names, but its displayed Bank & SSC batch date is 18 November 2025.
  • Adda247’s official Jaipur page showed batch dates of 16 and 23 November 2026. Both have passed as of this 24 November 2026 snapshot; obtain the next start date.
  • Power Mind lists banking, but the detailed facilities on its public site are largely general/SSC oriented; confirm SBI-specific faculty and mocks.
  • T.I.M.E.’s official Jaipur page lists BANK/SSC, but its current local SBI PO batch, branch address and teachers were not confirmed. Call before visiting.
  • Mahendra’s national catalogue lists SBI PO, but its Jaipur branch page currently shows no batches listed.
  • Google star ratings and institute testimonials are not used as the main ranking metric because they do not reliably measure the current SBI PO batch.
